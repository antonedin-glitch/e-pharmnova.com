Ninety percent of drug programs fail in the clinic. We start from molecules with known human safety, let AI find the ones that act on aging biology, and test them where we already run trials.

Large pharma is spending billions on AI laboratories. We take a different route: open models with commercial licences, cloud and on-premise NVIDIA GPUs, outsourced wet labs, and a rare asset that most AI-first biotechs lack — our own early-phase clinical infrastructure and, with informed consent, the longitudinal clinical and omics data it generates.

Aging itself is not an indication. Every program is framed around a concrete condition or a validated biomarker endpoint in a defined population, under ethics-committee and regulator approval.

Platform · Discovery AI

Discovery AI: a narrow, reproducible discovery engine

Four GPU-backed stages, orchestrated end to end in containers so every hit can be traced back to its data and model versions.

01 · TARGET

Target and signature discovery

LLM agents mine literature and public omics (Open Targets, GTEx, CELLxGENE, GenAge, DrugAge) to rank targets and senescence signatures.

02 · SCREEN

Structure, docking, signature reversal

Boltz-2 and OpenFold3 structures, GNINA / Vina / DiffDock docking, and CMap–LINCS signature reversal across approved and Phase I-tested libraries.

03 · FILTER

ADMET, safety, freedom to operate

ADMET-AI and RDKit filters, physics-based refinement (OpenMM, OpenFE) and medicinal-chemistry review reduce hundreds of hits to a shortlist.

04 · VALIDATE

Wet lab and clinic

Senescence assays and C. elegans lifespan studies at contract labs, then biomarker-driven pilots in our partner Phase I unit.

Compute & data

Where the GPUs sit

Daily inference — structure prediction, docking, generative chemistry — runs on an on-premise NVIDIA workstation. Peak screening campaigns burst to rented H100 / B200 capacity. Workflows run in Docker under Nextflow, with results and audit trails in an ELN so the data is IND-ready from day one.

Patient-derived data is processed in a segregated, de-identified environment compliant with Panama's Ley 81 de 2019 and, for US data, HIPAA.
